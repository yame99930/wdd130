Riverbend Rafting Co.

At Riverbend, every rapid is better shared. Our experienced guides bring people together for memorable days on the water, with safety, respect for the river, and a little splash of adventure at the heart of every trip.

History

Riverbend began with a borrowed raft, a stretch of river we loved, and a promise to show more people the joy of running it together. Since our first summer on the water, we have grown into a close-knit crew of guides who still believe the best stories start downstream.
